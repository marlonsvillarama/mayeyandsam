The venue details will be shared in your formal invitation. It will be somewhere beautiful on the island — Boracay has no shortage of stunning settings!
Boracay is a small island — most places are reachable by tricycle, e-bike, or a short walk. We'll include transport tips in your event details. If you're staying nearby, we may also arrange a group meetup point.
Dress Code
We're going all white and beige! Beach casual attire is very much encouraged — think linen shirts, sundresses, shorts, sandals, and light fabrics. You'll be on a tropical beach, so dress comfortably and embrace the island vibe. Just keep it in those white and beige tones.
Yes — the Welcome Dinner will be held outdoors, weather permitting. December is Boracay's dry season, so evenings are typically warm and breezy. Expect temperatures around 26–30°C. Light, breathable fabrics are your best friend, and sandals are very much welcome!
Travel & Boracay
We recommend arriving by December 25 or 26 to give yourself time to settle in and enjoy the island before the Welcome Dinner. Don't rush — arriving frazzled is never fun!
Fly into Caticlan Airport (MPH) — it's the closest airport to Boracay, just minutes from the port. Because Boracay is an island, getting there requires a land and sea transfer: a short drive to the port, followed by a bangka (outrigger boat) ride of roughly 15 minutes across to the island. Your hotel can arrange this transfer for you directly, so we recommend getting in touch with them ahead of your arrival.
Many nationalities, including Canadian citizens, can enter the Philippines visa-free for up to 30 days. We recommend verifying your specific country's requirements with the nearest Philippine embassy or consulate well in advance of travel.
Boracay has a wonderful range of accommodations at different price points — from boutique guesthouses to luxury beachfront resorts. The wedding ceremony and reception will be held at the Movenpick Resort & Spa, but you are absolutely not required to stay there. If you choose to stay elsewhere on the island, the Movenpick is only a quick e-tricycle ride away. Check the accommodations page of this website for some suggestions!

We wouldn't have it any other way! Expect a spread that showcases delicious Filipino flavours alongside other dishes. If you have food allergies or restrictions, please let us know in your RSVP.

Other Practicalities
The currency in the Philippines is the Philippine Peso (PHP). There are ATMs on the island, but we recommend exchanging some cash before arriving or at the airport in Manila. Many larger establishments also accept credit cards.
We recommend drinking bottled or filtered water during your stay, as tap water in the Philippines is generally not safe for drinking. Bottled water is widely available and inexpensive throughout the island — and your hotel may also provide complimentary bottled drinking water in your room.
