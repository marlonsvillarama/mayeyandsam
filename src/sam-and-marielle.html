Getting Here

Your journey
to Boracay.

Boracay is a small island that requires a short journey from the mainland. Here's how to get there from both Calgary and Manila.

1
Calgary (YYC) → Manila (MNL) Fly from Calgary International Airport to Ninoy Aquino International Airport. Most routes connect via Tokyo, Seoul (ICN), Hong Kong, or Vancouver. Airlines: Air Canada, Philippine Airlines, Korean Air, Cathay Pacific.
~16–20hwith stopover
2
Manila (MNL) → Caticlan (MPH) Fly from Manila to Godofredo P. Ramos Airport in Caticlan — the closest airport to Boracay. Several daily flights via Cebu Pacific, Philippine Airlines, or AirAsia. Alternatively, fly to Kalibo (KLO) for cheaper fares (longer transfer).
~1hflight
3
Caticlan Jetty Port → Boracay Island A short tricycle ride from Caticlan airport takes you to the jetty port. From there, take a bangka (outrigger boat) to Boracay's Cagban or Ilig-Iligan port.
~15 minboat ride
4
Boracay Port → Your Hotel / Venue E-trikes (electric tuktuks) operate island-wide from the port. White Beach is easily accessible from either port.
~10–20 minby e-trike
💡

Tip for Calgary guests: Book flights at least 3–4 months in advance for December travel. Arrive in Boracay on December 25–26 to settle in before the big day. Budget roughly CAD $1,600–$2,800 for round-trip airfare from Calgary depending on your airline and stopover city.

1
Option A — Fly: Manila (MNL) → Caticlan (MPH) The fastest option. Multiple daily flights from NAIA or Clark Airport on Cebu Pacific, Philippine Airlines, or AirAsia. Book early for the best fares, especially over the Christmas holiday season.
~1hflight
2
Option B — Bus + Ferry: Manila → Caticlan Take a bus from Manila to Roxas City or Iloilo, then a ferry to Caticlan. This is the budget option — takes significantly longer but is very affordable. Not ideal if joining right before the wedding.
~12–16htotal journey
3
Caticlan Jetty → Boracay Island From Caticlan airport or jetty port, board a bangka boat to Boracay. Boats run regularly throughout the day. Environmental fee of ₱100 and terminal fee of ₱100 apply.
~15 minboat ride
4
Cagban Port → Your Hotel / Venue E-trikes meet all incoming boats and run along the main road to all beach stations. White Beach (Station 1–3) is a short ride from Cagban port.
~10–20 minby e-trike
💡

Tip for Manila guests: December is peak season — book your Caticlan flights 2–3 months ahead. Cebu Pacific often has promos. Expect round-trip flights of around ₱2,500–₱5,500. Consider flying into Kalibo (KLO) as a budget alternative (add ~1.5h bus transfer to Caticlan).

Explore Boracay

Things to do
before the big day.

Arrive early and make the most of paradise. Boracay is packed with adventures and relaxation for every kind of guest.

🏄

Water Sports

Kitesurfing, parasailing, windsurfing, paddleboarding, and banana boat rides are all available along White Beach and Bulabog Beach.

Adventure
🤿

Island Hopping & Diving

Snorkel the vibrant coral reefs of Crocodile Island or dive the WWII-era Yapak Wall. Island hopping tours depart daily from the beach.

Nature
🌅

Sunset Sailing

Board a traditional paraw (outrigger sailboat) for a magical golden hour cruise along White Beach — a Boracay signature experience.

Romantic
🍹

D'Mall & Beach Bars

Explore D'Mall, the island's lively shopping and dining hub. Enjoy fresh seafood, mango shakes, and cocktails with sand between your toes.

Food & Drink
💆

Spa & Massage

Indulge in an affordable beachside massage or visit one of the island's wellness spas for a full relaxation treatment before the festivities.

Relaxation
🌊

Puka Shell Beach

Head north to Puka Shell Beach for a quieter, more rustic stretch of white sand away from the main tourist strip. Stunning and peaceful.

Hidden Gem
🪩

Nightlife

Boracay's fire dancers, beach parties, and clubs come alive after dark. Epic Bar and Bom Bom Beach Club are festive seasonal favourites.

Nightlife
🏔️

Ariel's Point

A full-day cliff diving and snorkelling excursion that includes all-you-can-eat BBQ and unlimited drinks on a remote island. A must-do.
